# Content sources register

Curated content lives in `server/content/catalogue.ts` and is published through the normal staff path (`server/content/sync.ts`). Every record carries a provenance label that the interface shows:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Verified | Checked against an official source (park, museum, government, organiser) on the review date |
| Reported | From secondary or travel sources; check locally |
| Staff | General guidance written by the STIS team |
| Illustrative | Demonstration record. Says "not a real …" and has no external booking link |

Facts are paraphrased; no text, photos or logos are copied. Prices, fares, timetables and availability are deliberately not stated. Where sources disagree (Bako boat fares, Semenggoh feeding times, the airline names for Mulu), the record says to check with the operator.
